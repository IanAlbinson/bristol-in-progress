How Bristol voted

The August 11, 2026 primary at three levels: all of Vermont, Addison County's 23 towns, and Bristol itself. Party ballots are separate in a Vermont primary, so every share below is a share of that party's ballots only.

Please note

This is my own page, not an official Town of Bristol publication, and nothing here speaks for the Selectboard or the Town Clerk. The certified results live with the Vermont Secretary of State and in the Bristol Town Clerk's office.

Sources. Town-by-town vote counts come from the Vermont Secretary of State's Official Report of the Canvassing Committee — United States and Vermont Statewide Offices, 08/11/2026 August Primary, certified August 17, 2026. Legislative and county races come from the Secretary of State's 2026 August Primary Winner Listing, which reports district and county totals only. Bristol's own figures, including its checklist count and its results in the district and county races, come from the Town of Bristol Official Return of Votes, signed by Town Clerk Sharon Lucia and Assistant Town Clerk Patricia Heck. Compiled September 7, 2026.

How the numbers were checked. Every town and county subtotal was reconciled against the Secretary of State's own totals, and all 28 races on Bristol's return reconcile to their stated totals. Percentages are of ballots cast in that party's primary. Where voters could choose two candidates, the base is twice that party's ballot count, which is how the State reports it. "Blank votes" are unused slots, so in a vote-for-two race a voter who marks one candidate and stops produces one blank.

On write-in names. Write-ins are public record on the Town's certified return. This page lists names that drew two or more votes, plus anyone who filed as a candidate for some office. Single write-in votes for other names are shown as a count rather than listed. The certified return, which records every write-in, is a public record held in the Town Clerk’s office.

Turnout is shown for Vermont as a whole and for Bristol, the two places with a published registered-voter count in these records. Other towns and counties have none here, so no turnout rate is shown for them.
