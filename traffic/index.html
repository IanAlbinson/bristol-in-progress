What the counts showed Winslow, Nov 2024

Truck volumes through the village are not excessive compared with other village streets, and ACRPC assessed a 25 mph limit for most of the village as reasonable (Plank Rd. and the northern end of North St. may be exceptions). As traffic volume rises from north to south on North St., speeds fall.

Sources

Traffic counts: ACRPC 2020 village survey and September 2024 North Street count, summarized in a Mike Winslow email dated November 7, 2024 (Bristol Selectboard packet 2024-12-16, item V.4). Crash points: Bristol crash dashboard, from the VTrans Crash Query Tool, downtown district, August 2020 through July 2025. Road geometry: OpenStreetMap. Study-area gateways: Bristol FY2027 Downtown Access Study request.

How the numbers were checked

Count figures are transcribed from Winslow's summary table. Crash points are plotted from the dashboard's own coordinates. Study corridors are traced along OpenStreetMap street centerlines from the main downtown intersection to each gateway. Four count locations without a crash-dashboard anchor (North St. @ ambulance, North St. @ Lathrop Ln., Plank Rd., Pleasant St.) are placed on the correct street and flagged “approx.” in their popups.

Data current to the sources above. Page compiled September 16, 2026.
