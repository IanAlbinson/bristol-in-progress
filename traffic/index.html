What the counts said (Winslow, Nov 2024)

• Truck traffic on North St. is not excessive compared to other village streets.
• A 25 mph limit for most of the village is “entirely reasonable” (Plank Rd. and the northern end of North St. may be exceptions).
• As volume rises north→south on North St., speeds drop.
• Liberty St. stands out on both speed and % trucks (likely town-garage proximity).

Sources & caveats

Data: ACRPC counts, 2020 village survey + Sept 2024 North St. count, summarized in M. Winslow email 2024-11-07 (Bristol SB packet 2024-12-16, item V.4). Study-area gateways per Bristol FY2027 Downtown Access Study request.

Locations: count pins and gateways are anchored to real coordinates from the Bristol crash dashboard dataset; corridors are traced through on-road crash waypoints. Four count spots have no dashboard anchor (North St. @ ambulance, North St. @ Lathrop Ln., Plank Rd., Pleasant St.) and are flagged “approx.” in their popups. The exact counter position on each street is still approximate. The 2024 North St. count is flagged; all others are 2020.
