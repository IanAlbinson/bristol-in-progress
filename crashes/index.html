Method

The Police District is the geographic area of the former Village of Bristol (24 V.S.A. App. ch. 108, § 108-701). The VTrans export has no district field. Crashes are classified by street name: addresses naming a village street read as District, addresses naming a known rural road read as Town, and anything giving only a route number ("VT-116", "VT Rt 17") or no road at all is left Unclassified rather than guessed.

Reporting agency is as recorded by VTrans. All VSP barracks are grouped as Vermont State Police; the barracks name is in each popup.
