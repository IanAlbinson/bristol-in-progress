Buildings and infrastructure

Holley Hall energy retrofit

Underway

Heating and efficiency work at Holley Hall, funded by a state Municipal Energy Resilience Program grant of $325,853.

Lawrence Memorial Library hazmat abatement

Underway

Asbestos and PCB removal ahead of the library renovation, paid for with ARPA funds. Confirmation sampling is still running.

Mountain Street stormwater

Scoping

Stormwater and drainage improvements on Mountain Street.

EV charging on the Town Green

Quote in hand

Level 2 public chargers at the Town Green. There's a quote on the table with a near-term expiration.

Fast EV charging

On hold

A DC fast charger for Bristol. Site and scope are still open, and it's waiting on utility interconnection.

Town technology at the Hub

Scoping

Network and IT work at the Hub Teen Center.

Getting around downtown

Downtown access and circulation study

Grant request

A regional planning grant request to study parking, walking and traffic downtown. Pulls in the West Street work and a possible 25 mph speed reduction. Pitch is September 16.

Police services

Town-wide police services

Board work session

Residents outside the village core pay for a police district they aren't in. The board is working through whether to put town-wide coverage to the voters, and in which year.

Fall Middlebury interns

Starting September

Two Middlebury College interns this fall, working on the town-wide police question: resident survey and analysis on one side, public information on the other.
