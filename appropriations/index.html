Bristol Appropriations — Nonprofit Financial Dashboard

Two views: organizations requesting a Town appropriation at the March 2, 2026 Annual Town Meeting (FY2027), and other local-area nonprofits that do not receive an appropriation. Each shows the latest IRS Form 990 financials from ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer.

Appropriations source: Town of Bristol FY2025 Annual Report, Articles 11–15. Financial source: ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer (IRS Form 990 extracts). Compiled June 21, 2026.

Reading the data. Financial figures are each org's most recent IRS Form 990 with a data extract (mostly FY2023; a few FY2024). Many file on a June or August fiscal year, so a FY2024 return may exist but not yet be extracted. Regional nonprofits (Middlebury, Colchester, etc.) serve all of Addison County, not Bristol alone, so their totals reflect their full operation. Verify: "Addison County Restorative Justice Services" maps to the registered entity Addison County Court Diversion & Community Justice Projects Inc. Small local groups marked No 990 on file are likely 990-N (e-postcard) filers or operate under a parent/church and file no public financials.

Officer comp is "compensation of current officers" as reported on the 990; $0 often means an all-volunteer board or comp reported elsewhere. Lawrence Memorial Library is a municipal town library and files no Form 990. Have-a-Heart Food Shelf operates through St. Ambrose Catholic Church. Addison County River Watch Collaborative and RSVP/Foster Grandparent are programs under parent organizations (ACRPC and a regional volunteer center, respectively) and have no standalone 990. Open Door Clinic, Bristol CORE, Bristol Little League, and Addison County Readers are active nonprofits that do not surface a public 990 extract (likely 990-N filers).
