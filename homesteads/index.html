Bristol's homes, homestead or not

If you own a home in Vermont and it's where you live, you file a homestead declaration every year. The grand list records which parcels have one. This map shows every parcel in town by that one fact.

Please note

This is my own page, not an official Town of Bristol publication, and nothing here speaks for the Selectboard. The parcel lines and grand list fields come from the Vermont Center for Geographic Information's statewide parcel layer. The Bristol Listers' grand list is the record.

Most declarations sit on residential parcels, but not all. A farmhouse or an apartment over a store can be someone's home too, so a few farm, commercial and miscellaneous parcels carry one. They're green on the map and counted in the last row above.

Where those owners get their mail

What the colors mean. Green: a homestead declaration is on file, whatever the category. Gold: a residential parcel (R1, R2, S1, S2 or MHL) with no declaration. Blue: every other category with no declaration, mostly the miscellaneous and commercial categories. Grey: not on the 2025 grand list at all (water, road right-of-way, and parcels the state couldn't match).

What is left out. The state's layer carries owner names, mailing addresses and assessed values for every parcel. None of that is reproduced here. Owner mailing locations appear above only as totals, never by parcel.

What this can't tell you. Whether a home without a declaration is rented, empty, a second home or lived in by an owner who didn't file. The grand list doesn't record that.
